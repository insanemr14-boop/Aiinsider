Gemini's strategic advantage is not the model, it is the distribution. It appears in Search, in Docs and Gmail, on Android and in Google Cloud, which means adoption inside a Workspace organization requires no procurement conversation at all. For IT buyers that removes most of the friction that usually stalls assistant rollouts.

Technically, the standout capabilities are context length and multimodality. Handing it a long video, a large log file or a full repository and asking questions across the whole thing is a genuinely different workflow from chunk-and-retrieve, and the API exposes the same capability.

Where it lags is consistency. On careful drafting and multi-step analytical tasks, output quality varies more than with the leading alternatives. Test it against your actual documents rather than trusting the headline context number.
