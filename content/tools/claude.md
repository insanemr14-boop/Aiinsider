Claude's positioning is narrower than ChatGPT's and that is deliberate. There is no image generator and no sprawling marketplace of user-built assistants. What you get instead is a model tuned for sustained reasoning over large inputs, and a set of features — Projects, Artifacts, MCP connectors — that assume you are doing real work rather than experimenting.

The long-context behavior is the practical differentiator. Feed it a full contract set, a research corpus or a repository and the quality of recall holds up further into the context than most alternatives. That matters more than benchmark position for anyone whose job is reading.

The tradeoff is throughput. Heavy users on paid individual plans routinely hit message limits, which pushes serious workloads toward the API or higher tiers. Budget for that before standardizing a team on it.
