Synthesia solves a specific corporate problem: compliance and product training videos go stale, and re-shooting them requires a presenter, a studio and a schedule. Replacing the presenter with an avatar turns a re-shoot into a text edit.

That is a real efficiency, and it is why adoption concentrates in learning and development rather than marketing. Internal audiences tolerate a synthetic presenter when the alternative is a slide deck. External audiences read the same avatar as a signal that the company did not care enough to film a person.

The quality gap is narrowing but has not closed. Lip sync and voice are good; micro-expression, gesture and timing are not, and the deficit becomes obvious past a couple of minutes. Script for short segments, use it where information transfer is the goal, and keep it away from anything that depends on trust.
